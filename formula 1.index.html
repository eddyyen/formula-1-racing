Introduction to F1

What is Formula 1?

Formula 1 (F1) is the highest class of international open-wheel single-seater super-car racing governed by the Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile (FIA). The word "Formula" refers to the set of strict technical and sporting rules all participants must follow.

2026 FIA Technical Reset

The 2026 Technical & Engine Revolution

Formula 1 introduces a monumental regulation change focused on increased electrical hybrid energy, 100% sustainable fuels, active wing aerodynamics, and lighter, nimbler chassis.

Active Aerodynamics

DRS is replaced by active front and rear wing flaps. Drivers toggle between Z-Mode (high downforce for cornering) and X-Mode (low drag for overtaking on straights).

100% Sustainable Fuel

Cars utilize advanced, fully synthetic bio-derived fuels created from non-food biomass or carbon capture technologies, targeting net-zero carbon operations.

350kW MGU-K Hybrid

With the complex MGU-H removed, electrical output leaps to 350kW (~470 hp), nearly tripling current battery assist power while maintaining a total 1,000+ horsepower threshold.

Nimbler & Lighter Cars

Wheelbase decreases by 200mm, width narrows by 100mm, and minimum total mass drops by 30kg down to 768kg, enhancing racecraft agility on narrow road circuits.
